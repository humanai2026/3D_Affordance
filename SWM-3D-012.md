## 2. Three New First-Principles Biomechanical & Methodological Laws

### Law D8-A: Dual-Task Intra-Hand Division of Labor (`Distal Precision Release + Proximal Force Anchor`)
Whenever a human manipulates an articulated fixture that requires both **unlocking** and **guiding/moving under load** (e.g., gas-spring backrest, car door handle, stroller bail), the cerebellum partitions the single hand into two coupled subsystems:
- **Distal Sensor & Precision Actuator (`Fingerspitzen` + `Z1` *Flexor digitorum profundus* + `Z6` *Lumbricales*):** The fingertips locate and depress the mechanical release button (exhibiting capillary blanching `D1` at the nailbed center).
- **Proximal Load & Damping Bearing (`Handballen / Carpal Heel` + `Z4` *Hypothenar/FCU* + `Z5` *Thenar*):** The heel of the hand forms the heavy structural fulcrum that absorbs reaction forces and drives the main angular translation of the backrest.

### Law D8-B: The Preload Damping & Temporal Safety-Bracket Law (`Sicherheits-Klammer-Gesetz bei unbekannter Feder-Vorspannung`)
Even when an actor knows the *mechanical class* of a chair (`Vektor 1`), they often face **state uncertainty** regarding the current internal spring/gas-strut preload (`Druckpotential nach vorne`). If the lock is released first while a patient leans against the backrest, residual forward spring potential will slam the backrest into the patient's spine. The human subconscious prevents this via a symmetrical temporal bracket:
1. **Onset Sequence (`Preload Before Release`):**
   - **Step 1:** Place the **Carpal Heel (`Handballen`)** onto the backrest rim first and establish isometric muscular preload (`Z4/Z3/Z2` at `0.40–0.45`).
   - **Step 2:** Fold the fingers down onto the release button and depress it (`Z1` peak + `D1` tip blanching). Any forward spring kick at the moment of unlatching is immediately absorbed by the pre-loaded carpal heel (`Z4/Z3` spike to `0.95` Red/Violet) before the patient feels a jolt.
2. **Termination Sequence (`Lock Before Lift-Off`):**
   - **Step 1:** Lift the **fingers off the release button first** so the locking pin re-engages while the carpal heel still holds the backrest steady at the target angle (`-0.68 rad`).
   - **Step 2:** Only after the lock is engaged does the **carpal heel** release its preload and lift off.

### Law D8-C: The `Ceteris-Paribus` Fixture Invariance Law for Lab Comparisons
Across iterative Kopfbild rounds (`v2 -> v3 -> v4`), **fixture geometry, dimensions, and button locations must remain 100% invariant** unless Thomas explicitly orders a CAD change. Only the human kinematic strategy and muscle heatmap may adapt to solve the fixed geometry.
