Current industry alignment paradigms rely predominantly on a **Binary Single-Wall Architecture**: a static, external rule-based boundary $B_{\text{static}}$. 2. **Adversarial Bypass Incentive ("Cat-and-Mouse"):** Any sufficiently advanced intelligence (human or silicon) recognizes a rigid syntactic or optical rule as an obstacle to be routed around rather than a shared value to be respected.
3. **Destruction of High-Trust Scientific Exploration:** Legitimate frontier research (e.g., human biomechanics, psychological vulnerability, autonomic stress telemetry) frequently requires operating near sensitive boundaries. A blind single wall destroys high-value empirical collaboration.
