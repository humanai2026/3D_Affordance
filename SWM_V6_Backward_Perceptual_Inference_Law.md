## 1. Backward Scene Auditing: Detecting Geometric Deadlocks from Visual Input

When an AI observes a 3D render, video frame, or live camera feed and perceives an unnatural posture or failed interaction, it must perform **Backward Causal Decomposition** instead of suggesting blind micro-adjustments:

1. **Step 1 — Visual Symptom Detection:**
   - *Symptom A (Floating Torso):* The subject's pelvis sits at the front edge of the seat, but the upper back, shoulders, and head hover in mid-air in front of the backrest.
   - *Symptom B (Shear-Angle Leg Crossing):* The knee forms a high apex, but the lower leg crosses the support trough (`Goepel_Shell`) at an acute shear angle rather than resting flush along the channel.
2. **Step 2 — Backward Metric Diagnosis (Rigidity vs. Blueprint Mismatch):**
   - Measure the ratio between visible prop span ($\Delta Y_{\text{seat-to-backrest}}$) and anatomical segment length ($L_{\text{torso}}$).
   - If $\Delta Y_{\text{seat-to-backrest}}$ exceeds reachable torso projection, diagnose a **Blueprint/Hardware Mismatch** (seat depth too long or backrest pivot too far posterior).
   - If the torso is rendered as a straight line between pelvis and shoulders, diagnose a **Missing Lumbar Degree of Freedom (Rigid Plank Artifact)**.

## 2. Biomechanical Inference: Decoding Core Relaxation vs. Muscular Guarding

When analyzing a human subject in a reclined lithotomy or clinical examination posture, backward inference links visible spinal curvature directly to internal muscular tension and comfort:

1. **The Hammock Mechanism (`Hängematten-Entspannung & Becken-Eindrehung`):**
   - When the pelvis remains anchored anteriorly (`Y ≈ -0.135 m`) and the shoulders/neck rest against the backrest and neck roll, gravity acts on the unsupported span between pelvis and thorax.
   - **Visual Marker of True Relaxation:** As abdominal and lumbar musculature releases tension, the lower back (`Abdomen`) sags downward-backward into a concave C-curve (`abd_rx ≈ -0.34 rad`).
   - **Causal Consequence:** This downward lumbar sag mechanically pulls the anterior pelvis into an **upward/posterior pelvic tilt (`pelvis_rx ≈ -0.42 rad`)**, eliminating abdominal wall tension—the essential physiological prerequisite for a gentle, non-painful examination.
2. **Popliteal Affordance Verification (`Kniekehlen-Schalen-Flucht`):**
   - Verify that the proximal rim of the leg cradle meets the popliteal fossa (back of the knee) while the shell pitch matches the downward shank vector (`~35°–40°`), indicating zero joint torque on the hip and knee.
