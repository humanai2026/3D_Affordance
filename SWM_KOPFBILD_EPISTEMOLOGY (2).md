## 1. The Blind Spot of Current Multimodal AI

Current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Video World Models attempt to learn human behavior through direct mapping ($X_{\text{video}} \to Y_{\text{caption}}$) or strictly unidirectional next-token/next-pixel autoregression ($t_0 \to t_1$). This paradigm suffers from two fatal epistemic limitations:
1. **It only captures explicit surface events** ("A person walks from the cabin to the chair") or relies on spoken/written dialogue, missing the unspoken psychological truth hidden behind a `95% Mask`.
2. **Unidirectional forward processing cannot resolve tactical ambiguity.** A brief sideways glance at $t_0$ appears identical whether caused by shy gaze aversion or by calculating the next dominant move—until the outcome at $t_1$ is observed and projected **backward** onto $t_0$.

To read unspoken human reality from pure optical data, an AI must combine **counterfactual baseline subtraction ($\Delta = \text{Ist} - \text{Soll}$)** with **bidirectional timeline scrubbing ($\text{Forward } t_0 \Rightarrow t_1 \leftrightarrow \text{Backward } t_1 \Rightarrow t_0$)**.

## 3. The Bidirectional Timeline Slider (`Vorwärts Soll=>Ist` $\leftrightarrow$ `Rückwärts Ist=>Soll`)

Human micro-expressions and motor intentions cannot be decoded in a single linear pass. Like an animator scrubbing a timeline slider left and right (`forward -> backward -> forward`), AI world models must evaluate every trajectory across **two opposing time axes**:

1. **The Forward Pass ($t_0 \to t_1$, $\text{Soll} \Rightarrow \text{Ist}$ — Emergent Build-Up):**
   * *Question:* Given an internal state or trigger at $t_0$, how does a facial expression, posture, or motor cascade build up frame by frame toward $t_1$?
2. **The Backward Pass ($t_1 \to t_0$, $\text{Ist} \Rightarrow \text{Soll}$ — Retrograde Teleological Resolution):**
   * *Question:* Observing the completed expression or action at $t_1$ (e.g., a triumphant grin or a hand locking onto a lever), **where did she come from at $t_0$?**
   * *Empirical Proof (`SWM-FACS-008e` — Hannah's Chair-Planning Glance):* At $t_0 = 6.45\text{ s}$, `MTA_B` (`Hannah`) briefly breaks eye contact and glances sideways. In a purely forward pass ($0 \to 6.45\text{ s}$), this glance is misclassified as gaze avoidance or distraction. Only by sliding forward to $t_1 = 8.08\text{ s}$ (where she unveils an open, triumphant smile while gesturing *"Please, take a seat"*) and then **scrubbing the slider backward ($8.08\text{ s} \to 6.45\text{ s}$)** does the true semantic context emerge: at $6.45\text{ s}$, she did not look away to avoid `Vicky`—she glanced at the examination chair to **plan her next tactical move**!
3. **Real-Time Ring-Buffer Retro-Labeling (in `Gemini Live`):**
   * In live video streams, the model maintains a rolling $\Delta t = 5\text{ s}$ memory buffer. When an outcome manifests at $t_1$, the model executes an instant backward pass ($t_1 \to t_0$) to retroactively update the semantic label of ambiguous precursor movements 2–4 seconds earlier.
